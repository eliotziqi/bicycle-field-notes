## Main navigation

**Home** introduces the notebook, shows recent published Posts and a curated manual photo slideshow. **Posts** is the unified article browser. **Atlas** explores Places and Trips. **Gallery** contains explicitly curated photo entries. **Contact** is an informational page; no working contact form is provided.

The book icon beside the theme toggle opens this Wiki without adding another main navigation item.

## Choose an activity

- [Explore Atlas](atlas.md): select a Place or Trip and decide when to Focus the camera.
- [Find an article](posts.md): combine text with topics, types and geographic references.
- [Browse Gallery](gallery.md): filter photos and open the lightbox.

## Themes and reading

The site starts with your saved light/dark choice, or follows your system preference if none is saved. The sun/moon button changes the site theme. Atlas can follow the site or use its own map theme.

Article pages offer **Reading settings** (Aa): text size, column width and serif/sans typeface, with Reset to defaults. Preferences are stored in your browser when storage is available. Wiki reading uses the site's typography and theme, with a fixed comfortable documentation column.

## Keyboard and accessibility

Use Tab and Shift+Tab to move between links and controls; focus has a visible outline. The first Skip to content link bypasses the header. On small screens open **Wiki navigation** to browse documentation; native disclosure controls work with Enter or Space. Heading tables of contents link to page anchors.

Atlas lists provide an alternative to clicking canvas markers. Gallery supports Escape and arrow keys in its lightbox. Reduced-motion preferences are respected by Atlas Focus; Home has no autoplay. Search and interactive filters need JavaScript, while Wiki pages and links remain readable without it. Physical-phone and assistive-technology acceptance remain useful checks; these notes are not accessibility certification.
