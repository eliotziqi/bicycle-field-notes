## Browse photos

[Gallery](/gallery/) shows only deliberately created Gallery entries, not every upload. Select categories to match any chosen category (OR). Clear Filters restores all entries. A photo can have multiple categories.

## Open the lightbox

Activate a photo to open its larger view with description, original-image link and a related published Post when available. Use left/right arrows to move through the current filtered photos. Escape or Close exits and returns focus to the photo link. Without JavaScript the image link opens the original instead.

## Homepage slideshow

Home uses only Gallery records whose **Show on homepage** flag is enabled. Previous/Next and arrow keys provide manual movement; there is no autoplay or animation. Empty and single-photo collections have appropriate fallback states.

## Photos and articles

An upload alone does not create a Gallery entry or homepage slide. Images embedded in article bodies also do not automatically enter Gallery. A Gallery record's related Post is optional; drafts or unavailable Posts produce no public related-article link. See [publishing photos](../publishing/media-gallery.md) for the separate authoring actions.
